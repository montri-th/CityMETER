What can I use this data for?

What this helps answer
Explore terrain and elevation in 3D to understand an area and prepare questions for on-site investigation.

Specialist source

Data publisher
FABDEM — Hawker et al. / University of Bristol; Copernicus DEM; Terrain Tiles — Mapzen / AWS Open Data
Source
FABDEM V1-2, Copernicus GLO-30 and Terrain Tiles have distinct roles. Distinguish elevation readings from the rendered 3D surface and check the source used at each location. FABDEM V1-2 is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; check each model’s terms before reuse, especially commercial use.
Data period
Versions and acquisition periods differ by DEM; tile-production dates are not survey dates.
Coverage
Coverage depends on the elevation model; source and resolution can vary by location
Level of detail
Elevation models and GLO-30 minus FABDEM differences; 3D rendering may smooth or exaggerate height
Read the references
  • FABDEM V1-2 — University of Bristol (opens a new tab)
  • Terrain Tiles — provenance and hosting (opens a new tab)

Know before use: DEM values are not a land survey or flood-risk assessment. Differences between models are not verified building or tree heights, and no-data is not zero metres.
