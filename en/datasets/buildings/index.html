Buildings: Footprints, Height & Estimated Floor Area

Turn available building data into a city model showing density and estimated floor area without specialist GIS skills.

Open the map ↗

Questions this data can help answer

  • Where are buildings concentrated, and how does their height vary across the area?
  • How can estimated floor area help identify places for a closer study?

How to start reading the data

Read building footprints alongside estimated height and floor area to understand development patterns. Check the data version and coverage before comparing places: some buildings may be missing and estimates may be inaccurate. Decisions about an individual building need records and field checks.

Know before use

Building coverage and derived values may be incomplete or inaccurate. Use them to study development patterns, then check records or field evidence for an individual building.
