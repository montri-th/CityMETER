Buildings: Footprints, Height & Estimated Floor Area

See the shape of a neighbourhood through building heights, density and estimated floor area.

Open the map ↗

Similar footprints can tell very different stories

A footprint alone can make a low-rise and a tower look alike. Height and estimated floor area add context for choosing sites and planning neighbourhood research.

Heights, storeys and floor areas include estimates. Check building records or survey the site before a building-specific decision.

Two questions to start with

  • Where do tall and low-rise buildings cluster?
  • Which areas deserve a closer site visit?

When comparing or citing the data

Building coverage and derived values may be incomplete or inaccurate. Use them to study development patterns, then check records or field evidence for an individual building.
