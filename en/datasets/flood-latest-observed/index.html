Read the observation date before the extent

The extent represents a particular observation. Read its date alongside the basin context, then identify places needing field updates or comparison with other event reports.

Satellite observations have timing and detection limits. This is not a live situation map, and blank areas do not establish safety.

  • When was the displayed extent observed?
  • Where are field updates needed?
