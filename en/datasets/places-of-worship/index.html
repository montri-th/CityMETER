How to start reading the data

Use the records to prepare local-context research and community conversations. Separate all records from those with coordinates: a missing point does not mean no place exists. The inventory includes active places and abandoned temples, while population per site does not establish capacity, attendance or residents’ religious affiliation.

Know before use

A missing point does not mean no place exists. Population per site is not capacity, attendance or religious affiliation.
