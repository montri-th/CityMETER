Government Budget & Disbursement

Follow budget allocations and disbursements, and see where records with a geographic match are located.

Open the map ↗

Allocation and disbursement answer different questions

The adjusted budget shows the allocation; disbursement shows money drawn down. Read both within the same fiscal year, then choose the project records you want to investigate further.

The map includes only geographically matched records. Spending alone does not establish better services or value for money.

Two questions to start with

  • Which allocations and disbursements are mapped to the area?
  • How do those amounts compare within the same fiscal year?
