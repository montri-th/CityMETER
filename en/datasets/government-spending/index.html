Government Budget & Disbursement

Compare budget allocations and recorded disbursements by area to prepare questions about public spending.

Open the map ↗

Questions this data can help answer

  • How do adjusted budgets and recorded disbursements compare in matched areas?
  • What follow-up questions do the fiscal-year figures raise about projects?

How to start reading the data

Read adjusted budgets separately from recorded disbursements for the selected fiscal year. The map contains only items matched to areas, not total national spending. Use the figures to prepare project follow-up questions: allocation or disbursement alone does not establish outcomes, service quality or value for money.

Know before use

Mapped items are not total national spending. Allocation and disbursement are different stages; amounts alone do not establish outcomes, service quality or value for money.
