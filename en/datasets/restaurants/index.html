How to start reading the data

Read restaurant categories, price ranges and review scores together to frame local competitive research. Check collection dates and coverage before comparing neighbourhoods. Restaurant or review counts are not customer numbers or demand, and review scores are not an independent quality assessment.

Know before use

Restaurant and review counts are not demand or customer counts; source and collection date are not documented
