Questions this data can help answer

  • What advertised apartment-rent ranges appear in the neighbourhood?
  • How can building distribution and rent ranges help shortlist areas to investigate?

How to start reading the data

Read advertised rent ranges alongside the distribution of apartment buildings to understand the available neighbourhood sample. The records do not represent the entire rental market or establish current rent for every building. Follow up on the properties of interest before relying on a comparison.

Know before use

Do not present this as the entire rental market or as current rent for every building
