A hotspot is a starting point for verification

Detection locations and times show where thermal signals were observed. Compare them with land use and reports from responders or local people before concluding what occurred.

VIIRS/FIRMS hotspots are not all fire incidents, burned area or PM2.5 readings, and they are not evacuation guidance.

Two questions to start with

  • Where and when were hotspots detected?
  • Which detections have local reports to investigate?
