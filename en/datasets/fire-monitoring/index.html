How to start reading the data

Read hotspot locations with area context and detection times to choose where further checks are needed. Satellite detections do not represent every wildfire, burned area or PM2.5 levels. Consult field reports before drawing conclusions about the situation.

Know before use

Hotspots are satellite detections, not every wildfire, burned area or PM2.5 measurement. Read detection time and check field reports.
