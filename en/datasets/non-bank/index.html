Distinguish service locations before counting a network

Branches, offices, headquarters and companies are different units. Keeping them distinct makes network comparisons useful, with licence information supporting provider-specific checks.

No matched licence does not mean unlicensed. Branch counts do not measure lending volumes or demand.

When comparing or citing the data

An unmatched name or missing licence chip does not mean unlicensed. Licence categories can overlap. Branch counts are not loan volume, credit demand or debt risk.
