Questions this data can help answer

  • How are the recorded non-bank lender branches and offices distributed?
  • How should branches, companies and licence categories be separated before comparing networks?

How to start reading the data

Study the service network by keeping branches, offices, headquarters and companies distinct. Some coordinates are missing and licence categories can overlap; an unmatched name does not mean unlicensed. Branch counts describe the recorded network, not loan volume, credit demand or debt risk.

Know before use

An unmatched name or missing licence chip does not mean unlicensed. Licence categories can overlap. Branch counts are not loan volume, credit demand or debt risk.
