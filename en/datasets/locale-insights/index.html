How to start reading the data

Use locale profiles as starting context for service planning, community conversations and fieldwork priorities. The initial view is Bangkok; match locale_id to verified administrative or service boundaries before aggregation. The profiles do not establish official population, observed behaviour, eligibility, statutory boundaries or risk.

Know before use

Use as contextual input for service planning, field validation, engagement and prioritisation. Link locale_id to verified administrative or service boundaries before aggregation. It is not official population, observed behaviour, eligibility or risk determination.
