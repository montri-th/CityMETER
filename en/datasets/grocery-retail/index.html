Questions this data can help answer

  • How are the recorded retail and wholesale chains distributed across the area?
  • How can branch-count shares and store types help prioritise market visits?

How to start reading the data

Use stores, brands and store types to study the recorded retail, wholesale and pharmacy networks. Any count share describes this selected inventory, not sales, revenue share or observed demand. Check operating status and sales-floor area, which is estimated by store type, before planning.

Know before use

Store counts and count shares are not sales, revenue share or observed demand. Verify operating status and floor area before planning.
