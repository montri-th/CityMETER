Tourism Demand: Visitors & Spending

Compare visitor and spending trends across provinces when planning a tourism business or local activity.

Open the map ↗

Read visitor numbers alongside tourism receipts

Provinces with high visitor numbers can have different spending patterns. Compare the same period and consider seasonality and attractions before choosing a market to study.

Visitor counts need not represent unique people and can be revised. Provincial totals do not establish demand at an individual site.
