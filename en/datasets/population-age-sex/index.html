Questions this data can help answer

  • What is the age and sex structure of the registered population in the area?
  • How do age profiles compare between areas for the same reference month?

How to start reading the data

Use the same reference month when comparing age and sex profiles for service planning. These are registered-population figures, not a count of people actually present or daytime population. They do not establish income, service eligibility or local behaviour.

Know before use

This is not daytime population, income, service eligibility, or evidence of local behaviour
