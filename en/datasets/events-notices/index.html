How to start reading the data

Read available report categories, locations and times to identify evidence relevant to local work. Several reports may refer to one event, so report counts are not unique events or traffic volume. Coverage varies by import period, and finding no report does not establish that nothing happened.

Know before use

Report counts are not unique events or traffic volume. An early historical record does not prove continuous coverage, and no report does not mean no event.
