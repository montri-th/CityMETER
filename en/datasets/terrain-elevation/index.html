Terrain & Elevation

Explore terrain and elevation in 3D to understand an area and prepare questions for on-site investigation.

Open the map ↗

Questions this data can help answer

  • How do terrain shape and elevation vary across the area of interest?
  • What can differences between elevation models tell us, and what needs a field check?

How to start reading the data

Start with terrain shape, then check the model and resolution at the location. The 3D view may smooth or exaggerate elevation, and GLO-30 minus FABDEM is not a verified building or tree height. Use it to prepare field questions, not as a survey or flood-risk assessment; missing data is not zero metres.

Know before use

DEM values are not a land survey or flood-risk assessment. Differences between models are not verified building or tree heights, and no-data is not zero metres.

Sources and coverage

Specialist source

Data publisher
FABDEM — Hawker et al. / University of Bristol; Copernicus DEM; Terrain Tiles — Mapzen / AWS Open Data
Source
FABDEM V1-2, Copernicus GLO-30 and Terrain Tiles have distinct roles. Distinguish elevation readings from the rendered 3D surface and check the source used at each location. FABDEM V1-2 is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; check each model’s terms before reuse, especially commercial use.
Data period
Versions and acquisition periods differ by DEM; tile-production dates are not survey dates.
Coverage
Coverage depends on the elevation model; source and resolution can vary by location
Level of detail
Elevation models and GLO-30 minus FABDEM differences; 3D rendering may smooth or exaggerate height
