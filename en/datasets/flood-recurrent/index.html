Flood: Recurrent

Look back at areas where flooding was detected across multiple years to inform land-use questions and wet-season preparation.

Open the map ↗

Where has flooding been detected repeatedly?

The number of years with detections shows a historical pattern. Read the selected period alongside local conditions and community experience to choose places for drainage or preparedness follow-up.

Past detections are not current conditions, a forecast or a statutory risk boundary. An area without records is not necessarily flood-free.
