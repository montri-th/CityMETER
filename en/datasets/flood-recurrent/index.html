Flood: Recurrent

Compare areas where flooding was observed in past years before studying a location further.

Open the map ↗

Questions this data can help answer

  • Where was flooding detected in several years of the selected period?
  • How can year-by-year flood history guide further location research?

How to start reading the data

Read yearly observed flood extents with the number of years showing detections in the selected period. The history helps identify places for further study, but it does not describe current conditions, predict the next flood or replace a statutory risk determination.

Know before use

Historical recurrence is neither current conditions nor a forecast and does not replace a statutory risk determination
