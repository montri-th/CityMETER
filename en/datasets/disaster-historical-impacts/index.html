Disaster: Historical Impacts

Turn village-level disaster history into a map of past impacts to support evidence-based preparedness.

Open the map ↗

Questions this data can help answer

  • Which areas recorded disaster impacts in the years of interest?
  • How can past impacts guide preparedness questions and follow-up checks?

How to start reading the data

Compare reported impacts by area and year to frame further preparedness research. Check the reporting unit before counting events, since one event may span several rows. Historical impacts are neither current conditions nor a forecast; read them with their period and reporting limits.

Know before use

Historical impacts are not current risk; period, source, and reporting limitations still need documentation
