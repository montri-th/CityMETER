Disaster: Historical Impacts

Learn from village-level disaster and impact reports when preparing communities for future events.

Open the map ↗

Past events help frame preparedness questions

Read hazard type and year alongside reported effects on people, households, homes, livelihoods or infrastructure. Use that history to choose topics for community and agency discussions.

The reports cover 2014–2024. One event may produce several records, so check the counting basis before adding totals. This is not a risk rating or forecast.

Two questions to start with

  • Which hazards and impacts have been reported here?
  • Which experiences should inform preparedness discussions?
