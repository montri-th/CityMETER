Municipal Revenue

Compare local-government revenue over time to prepare better questions about public resources.

Open the map ↗

Questions this data can help answer

  • How do own-source revenue, allocated revenue and grants contribute to each local authority’s income?
  • How can annual, per-person and per-area figures guide budget-planning questions?

How to start reading the data

Read revenue components and fiscal-year trends before comparing local authorities. Per-person and per-area figures are CityMETER calculations, so check their denominators. Revenue can frame questions about public resources but does not establish service quality or overall fiscal capacity.

Know before use

Revenue is not overall service quality or fiscal capacity; read it with the accounting year and denominator definitions
